# RetailCo Executive MIS Dashboard FY 2024-25

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the development of a comprehensive Executive Management Information System (MIS) Dashboard using Microsoft Excel. The solution provides business insights through data cleaning, KPI generation, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and interactive dashboards.

The project was designed to support management decision-making by monitoring sales performance, purchase activities, profitability, payment collection status, and overall business performance.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing

* Removed duplicate records
* Standardized date formats
* Handled missing values
* Created helper columns (Month, Year, Week, Quarter)
* Validated Revenue, Cost, and Gross Profit calculations

### 2. KPI Development

Developed business KPIs including:

* Total Revenue
* Total Cost
* Gross Profit
* GP Margin %
* Collection Rate %
* Top Product
* Top Category
* Top Region
* Top Vendor
* Best Performing Month
* Best Performing Week

### 3. Dashboard Development

#### Executive Summary Dashboard

Provides a management-level overview of business performance.

#### Monthly Business Performance Dashboard

Tracks monthly revenue, cost, profitability, and MoM growth.

#### Weekly Business Performance Dashboard

Monitors weekly business performance and WoW growth.

#### Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard

Analyzes sales by product, category, region, payment mode, payment status, and sales representative.

#### Purchase Analysis Dashboard

Analyzes procurement trends, vendor performance, purchase categories, products, and payment status.

---

## Key Insights

* Electronics generated the highest revenue contribution.
* LG Washing Machine 7kg emerged as the top-selling product.
* West region generated the highest sales revenue.
* Samsung India Pvt Ltd was the leading vendor by purchase cost.
* Collection rate exceeded 85% of total revenue.
* Revenue and profitability trends were monitored across monthly and weekly periods.
